Non-drug Treatments

We use an integrative approach to migraine management that includes both medication and non-medication therapies. When appropriate, we coordinate referrals to trusted providers in our network.

  • Acupuncture
  • Behavioral Interventions
  • Biofeedback
  • Chiropractic
  • Green Light Therapy
  • Lifestyle Factors
  • Neuromodulator devices
  • Supplements
  • Physical Therapy
  • Sleep health
  • Lidocaine SPG Blocks

Pharmaceutical Treatments

Preventative Options:

  • Anti-epileptics
  • Anti-depressants
  • Anti-hypertensives
  • Botox

  • Others

  • Procedures: trigger point injections & nerve blocks
