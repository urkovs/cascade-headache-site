Four marks, each built from computed geometry: consistent stroke weights, matched join angles, arcs that terminate on the lines they meet, and controlled curve amplitudes. Every one passes a 16px favicon legibility check.

A. Summit crown

A head drawn as a single circle whose crown rises into one peak, with the join angles matched so the peak grows out of the head instead of sitting on it. The whole story of the practice in one shape: the mountain lives where the headache lives. The most badge-like and the strongest favicon.
