We get it, because we’ve been there.

At Cascade Headache, we understand what it's like to miss work, cancel plans, and feel like your own body is working against you.

That's exactly why we created this practice:

A clinic built to serve patients with complex needs that other providers find challenging.

We work with you to uncover the factors at play that contribute to your headaches. Then we build a plan that is perfect for your life—no ‘one size fits all’ approach here.

Convenient Care Options:

Secure telemedicine appointments
Most consultations and follow-ups are done virtually. When your care plan calls for in-person procedures, we schedule dedicated clinic days in Lacey, WA.
Evening and weekend availability
We work with your schedule, even if that means having appointments after 5pm or on weekends.
In-person procedures
We perform Botox for Chronic Migraine, Occipital Nerve Blocks, Supraorbital & Supratrochlear Nerve Blocks, Trigger Point Injections, Masseter Injections, and Rescue Injections. If these are needed, we work with you to find convenient times to meet.
